Unlock smarter prompts (free, 1 minute)

A key lets Gemini ask you follow-up questions first, so your prompt starts with the full picture. It's free and takes about a minute:

  1. Open Google AI Studio and sign in with any Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key. Accept the terms if it asks.
  3. Copy the key it shows you.
  4. Come back, paste it below, tick Remember my key, and tap Check key.

Optional: lock your key to this site

If your key ever leaked, it would only work here. In Google Cloud Credentials, open your key. Under Application restrictions choose Websites and add https://kmagno94.github.io/*. Under API restrictions choose Generative Language API. Save.

Your key goes only to Google, never to this site. Remembering saves the key only, never your words. On Google's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products.

How it works

  1. Dump it. Type or talk. No need to be clear.
  2. Answer a few taps. With a key, Gemini asks what's missing.
  3. Paste it. Copy your prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any AI.

Talk it out runs the Speak to Prompt shortcut: record in Voice Memos, copy the transcript, and it brings you back here to paste. Afterward it takes you back to Voice Memos to move or delete the recording.

No shortcut? Use Voice Memos by hand
  1. Open Voice Memos and tap the red button. Ramble as long as you want, then tap it again to stop.
  2. Tap the three dots on your recording (or press and hold it), then Copy Transcript. If it isn't there yet, wait a few seconds for the transcript to finish.
  3. Come back here, press and hold the text box, and tap Paste.

Needs iOS 18 or later on an iPhone 12 or newer. The transcript is made on your phone.
